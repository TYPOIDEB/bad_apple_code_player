STEP 1 — DOWNLOAD GOOGLE AUTHENTICATOR

Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.authenticator2
iPhone: https://apps.apple.com/app/google-authenticator/id388497605

Open it after install.

STEP 2 — SCAN THE QR

Run BadAppleUnlock.exe. A window opens with a QR code.

On your phone:
1. Open Google Authenticator
2. Tap "+"
3. Tap "Scan a QR code"
4. Point camera at the QR on your screen

If QR won't scan, tap "+" → "Enter a setup key":
- Name: VideoUnlock
- Key: the string under the QR
- Type: Time-based

STEP 3 — HOW TO PLAY

Video plays 5 seconds, then stops. Enter the code from your phone
to unlock the next 5 seconds. Repeat until the end.

Space — pause
↑ / ↓ — volume
Esc — quit
Backspace — erase digit
Mouse — pause button + volume slider
